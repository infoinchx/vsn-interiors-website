## Four-world edition additions

50 original interior concepts are included in public/images/generated as full-resolution WebP files, alongside smaller thumbnails. GENERATED-IMAGE-PROMPTS.json records their prompts and intended room categories. The website labels these as AI design concepts. They are not claimed as completed Vsn work.

The built-in image generation tool produced the new concepts. The site asset pipeline encodes them for efficient delivery, preserving their composition. No external image hosts are required when browsing the website.

## Distinct-page edition additions

12 new original AI interior concepts and one fictional Ganesh demo portrait are in public/images/edition3. EDITION3-IMAGE-PROMPTS.json documents these and the generated transparent architectural logo symbol. The portrait is labelled fictional on the About page. The SVG logo export embeds the original PNG symbol and uses Arial text; it is not fully vector artwork. The gallery now contains 62 AI concepts and 8 stock designs.
